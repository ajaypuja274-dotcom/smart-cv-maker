Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Information you enter

The current Smart CV Maker version creates your resume in your browser. Draft autosave, where available, uses local browser storage on your device. Do not use a shared device if you do not want another person using that browser to see your saved draft.

Photo uploads

A profile photo is read by your browser to display it in the resume preview. The current static version does not upload it to a Smart CV Maker server.

Third parties and advertising

No advertising or analytics service is configured in this release. If advertising, analytics, login, hosting, or other third-party services are added later, this policy must be updated to explain the data they collect and how they use it.

Your choices

You can clear the form and clear this browser's site data to remove locally stored draft information. PDF printing is handled by your browser or operating system.

Contact

Add the website owner's valid contact email before launch.
